Demo mode: traffic is synthetic (attributed to processes that really have connections open), and rules are simulated. Nothing on your system is changed.
Download limits are off: the WinDivert driver isn't installed. Blocking and upload limits work without it. To turn download limits on:
  1. In Windows Security, go to Virus & threat protection > Manage settings > Exclusions > Add an exclusion > Folder, and choose C:\Program Files\NetLeash\windivert. Defender deletes WinDivert otherwise, because malware uses it too; NetLeash won't change Defender settings for you.
  2. Run Install NetLeash.exe from the NetLeash package again and answer Y. Your rules are kept.
